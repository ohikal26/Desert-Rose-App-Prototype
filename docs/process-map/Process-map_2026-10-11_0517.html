Desert Rose OE system: how it works

One problem list connects shift checklists, inspections and guest feedback to the people who fix them, and to what leaders see in the Excellence Gateway. Prototype with made-up data.

1. Capture: where problems come from

Shift checklistsPool & beach attendant, room attendantScan the place's QR code (or pick from a list). Tick each item, or mark "could not do". Report a problem from any item, with a photo.
InspectionsOE team (auditor)Two marks per item: were the steps followed, and is the result good? Any item can be added as a problem, with the note and photo carried over.
Guest feedbackHeads, directors, OE team, in the GatewayConcerns from the guest survey, in-house feedback, Booking.com, HolidayCheck and others. "Add as a problem" fills in the place, kind and wording.
Seen on the spotAnyoneReport a problem: what, where, a photo, condition or care, and whether it is a safety risk.
↓ all go into one problem list, with the same rules ↓

2. Fix: the problem loop

ReportedPlace, photo, kind. A safety risk needs a temporary measure first, and goes to the top of every list.
→
AssignedSupervisor, manager or OE teamOne owner and a due date. The owner sees it on their badge.
→
FixedThe ownerFixes it and marks it fixed.
→
ConfirmedSomeone else: supervisor, manager or OE team for that areaNever the person who fixed it. Not fixed? It goes back to the owner.
→
ClosedCounts toward "closed on time" and typical days to fix.
OverdueAutomatic when the due date passes. Shown in red to the owner, supervisor and leaders.
Capex listOnly condition problems in old areas. Leaves the team's open count and goes to the Owner's list.
Checklist sent backThe supervisor checks each finished checklist: confirm, or send back with a note.
Works offlineEverything saves on the device and is kept when the connection drops.
↓ the same counts and words feed every view ↓

3. Review and measure: who looks at what, and when

SupervisorThrough the shiftChecklists to check, fixes to confirm, problems to assign.
→
DirectorEvery morningDepartment overview: safety, today's checklists, fixes not yet confirmed, how long problems have waited, where they cluster, 9-week trend.
→
Morning briefingEach department, daily (Gateway)One page: scores against target, today in the OE app, guest concerns from yesterday and this month, issues to discuss.
→
GM, CEO, OwnerGM daily · CEO daily and weekly · Owner weeklyLeadership views: safety, what needs a push, the weekly trend, the capex list, progress by building.
→
Gateway dashboardLeaders, monthly OKR cycleOverall score and four perspectives: financial, guest experience, operational excellence, people & culture. Each key result against target.
Back into the loopAnything a leader or briefing raises is added as a problem with an owner and a due date, so it is followed up in the same way.
Scorecard (draft, for leadership to decide)Bonuses could later be based on a few outcome key results per department: trusted guest scores, OE audit quality, cost, retention. Measured by team, never by person.
Never in the scorecardProblems reported or open, checklists ticked, a team's checks on its own work, any one person's numbers. These would reward hiding problems instead of fixing them.
Rules: safety first · one owner per problem · you cannot confirm your own fix · overdue is automatic · counts by place and kind, never by person · English and ArabicDesert Rose OE prototype · 11 Oct 2026
